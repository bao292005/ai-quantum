## The central story (pivot)

The MPS tensor-network fragility model was fully specified (3R.1) and built
(Epic 2–3) before it was tested on real crisis data. The Story 4.1 de-risk spike
then showed it **did not discriminate** crisis from calm (normal mean 0.337 >
LUNA 0.269 — inverted). A trivial **B0 borrow-rate** detector met the Success
Signal easily (LUNA RED ~27 h early, FP 0% on the control), so B0 was adopted as
the v1 detector and MPS was parked (`research/decision_b0_v1_detector.md`).

## What went well

- **De-risk spike (4.1) prevented building on sand** — caught the non-discriminating
  model before investing in calibration/multiprocessing/API.
- **Test-first with real oracles** throughout (MPS oracles verified via the real
  code path; Success Signal encoded as a regression test).
- **Right-sizing** — Epic 3 deferred 3B/3D (baseline 290× under gate); Epic 4
  deferred IPC (B0 not CPU-bound). No gold-plating.
- **Decision records** for every major call — easy to trace "why".

## What didn't / lessons

- **Built the MPS model fully before validating its signal.** 3R.2 flagged the
  novelty risk (R1); the discrimination spike should have run right after
  `fragility_raw` + a fixture existed, not after all of Epic 3.
- **Unenforced contract assumption** — 3R.1 assumed "X is normalized" but nothing
  applied it; `feature_tensor` emitted raw ~1e18 volume → F_raw ≈ 0 flat, only
  surfaced at 4.1. Assumptions must be enforced by a test/guard, not a comment.
- **Success Signal partial** — LUNA proven, FTX is a documented false-negative.

## Honesty debts (open)

1. **FTX = false-negative** (off-chain origin, tiny on-chain footprint). B0 covers
   only on-chain high-severity (LUNA-class).
2. **FP = 0% rests on a single normal fixture** — not a general FP rate.
3. **v1 semantics = "elevated-risk regime" alert, not a pinpoint** (~27 h lead).
4. **Code-review M1** — the FP assertion in `test_baseline_success_signal.py` is
   tautological (threshold = max(normal)), so it protects nothing.
5. **No full realtime orchestrator** — `evaluate_and_emit` is a step, not yet wired
   into the WSS loop (1E.1 stops at the ring buffer).

## Verdict (Project Lead: bao)

**v1 is NOT ready to ship — close the FTX gap first.** LUNA-class coverage alone
is insufficient; catching FTX-class (off-chain-origin) cascades is a ship gate.
